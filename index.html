03Customer support workflows & response systemsBUSINESS ANALYSIS+

The challenge

Support teams need responses that are consistent, relevant to the customer's situation and connected to clear resolution paths.

My contribution

Created reusable response templates by anticipating customer behaviour and common scenarios, organised escalation and support processes, and explored ERP/CRM-enabled workflows for more timely support.

What to measure

Response time, first-contact resolution, repeat-contact rate, template adoption and escalation volume—only where reliable historical data exists.

04AI-powered saree virtual try-on conceptAI / LEARNING+

The opportunity

Online shoppers may find it difficult to imagine how a saree looks when worn. A virtual try-on experience could help bridge the gap between product photography and a more contextual preview.

Current direction

Learning Python foundations and progressing toward computer vision, image processing and virtual try-on research. The concept is presented as work in progress, not a production-ready AI system.

Next milestones

Define user requirements; create a small, consented test dataset; evaluate existing research and models; build a baseline prototype; document quality, limitations and evaluation criteria.
